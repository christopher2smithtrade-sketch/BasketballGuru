PRESEASON DRY RUN -- exhibition slate. Starters play 20 minutes and coaches experiment, so treat the numbers as the pipeline warming up; the real board starts on opening night.

The headline is projected Points + Rebounds + Assists for the night he plays (Pick6 voids a DNP). It is built bottom-up: projected minutes × what he produces per minute (both seasons, recent games weighted up) × what tonight's opponent gives up to his position. Then every team is made to add up: minutes to 240 plus a little overtime, and points to what DraftKings' spread and total say the team will score. When a starter is ruled out, his minutes and his share of the shots go to the players left -- the backups at his position first.

BOOST is the projection minus his last-10 PRA average. The last-10 average, to the half point, is roughly where a book with no news hangs the line, so a big boost means tonight is unusual: a teammate out, a pace-up spot, a soft matchup, more minutes. MORE and LESS tags mark boosts of 3+ either way. In the 2025-26 backtest, players with a +4 boost cleared their last-10 line about 73% of the time and players at -4 or lower stayed under it about 73% of the time. Real DK lines react to news, so check the line before you trust a boost.

Line checker: type the DK PRA line on any card for the More / Less percentage. The ladder and the range use the same distribution, fitted to how far real games land from the projection.

The scorecard at the bottom is the honesty check: every board is logged at tip-off and graded the next morning against the box scores -- PRA error, whether the grades sort players, and whether the MORE / LESS calls beat a coin flip.

Data: ESPN (schedule, box scores, rosters, injuries, DraftKings spreads and totals). No paid feeds.
