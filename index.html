Simple or Detailed: Simple cards (the default) put the answer first -- a verdict, DraftKings' line vs our projection, and the reasons in plain words. Detailed cards show every number. The switch is at the top of the board, and your phone remembers your choice.

The headline is projected Points + Rebounds + Assists for the night he plays (a prop on a player who sits is voided). It is built bottom-up: projected minutes × what he produces per minute (both seasons, recent games weighted up). Then every team is made to add up: minutes to 240 plus a little overtime, and points to what DraftKings' spread and total say the team will score. When a starter is ruled out, his minutes and his share of the shots go to the players left -- the backups at his position first. The opponent counts through those team totals (pace, points, rebounds and assists it allows); there is no defense-vs-position adjustment, because in the 2025-26 backtest it added nothing.

DK line is DraftKings' actual PRA line, pulled free from ESPN for every player DK prices. The number next to it is the model's disagreement: projection minus line. MORE and LESS tags mark disagreements of 3+.

Be careful with the tags. Tested against 1,179 real DK lines from last season (mostly playoffs), DraftKings was more accurate than the model (off by 6.2 a player vs 6.5), and when the model disagreed by 3+ it was right only 49% of the time. So the percentages on this board lean on DK's line -- the model gets a 10% say -- and the tags are the model's opinion, graded every night on the scorecard. Trust them once the scorecard shows them winning well above 50%, not before. Players DK does not price fall back to their recent average as the line (marked "vs avg").

⚠ Check the news appears when the model and DraftKings are 6+ points apart (4+ if he changed teams, missed time mid-season, or has no recent NBA games). Gaps that big usually mean DraftKings knows something the injury report doesn't -- a bench role, a minutes limit -- so look it up before trusting the model. On those cards the odds use DraftKings' line alone.

Line checker: each card is filled in with DK's line and shows the More / Less percentage. To check a different line, type it in. The ladder and the range use the same numbers.

The scorecard at the bottom is the honesty check: every board is logged at tip-off and graded the next morning against the box scores -- PRA error, whether the grades sort players, the model vs DK's own line on the same players, and whether the MORE / LESS calls beat a coin flip.
